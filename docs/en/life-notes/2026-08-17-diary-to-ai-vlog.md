A diary feels important while it is being written, but it is rarely read again. Dates and sentences accumulate in storage, and it is difficult to find a way for those records to return to daily life.

I began asking AI to turn diary entries into VLOG scripts. The goal was not simply to make videos faster. I wanted to see what would become visible when a past day appeared in another format.

## From diary to script

Diary entries contain sentences that only the writer understands. Context is omitted, emotion changes suddenly, and an important event may disappear after a few lines. AI helped group repeated events and feelings into a possible structure for a script.

The story selected by AI was not automatically the truth of the day. AI can favor dramatic events or combine several emotions into one lesson. The original entry therefore remained beside the draft while I asked whether the selected event had really mattered to me.

## The day looked different as a video

A scene that seemed minor in writing could remain in memory when presented visually. A worry that felt large on the day could pass quickly within the complete story.

This process was less about making memory attractive and more about discovering editing criteria. Choosing what to keep and remove revealed which moments I considered important.

## Personal knowledge gained an output

A knowledge base becomes heavy when it only collects. VLOG created a small exit: read the diary, review the script, make the video, and use the finished video to look back again.

AI’s role was not to interpret a life on the writer’s behalf. It found forgotten sentences and suggested possible connections. A person still decided what could be shared and what meaning to attach.

## Decide the right not to publish first

A diary contains stories about family and other people. Identifying details should be removed before publication, and the writer should verify whether the information is appropriate to send to an AI service.

The most important result was not the number of videos. It was gaining another moment to look at a day that would otherwise pass. The record changed slightly from a storage tool into a tool for reading life again.
